# Review: server-startup deadline

## Spec review, round 1

Verdict: CHANGES REQUESTED

- S1-1 [blocking] Qualify the causal claims and distinguish recorded evidence from reported measurements (spec "What I checked" and "Cause (likely, not proven)"). The heading acknowledges uncertainty, but "The children are not hanging; they are slow", "tsx compiles that graph on every start", and "any extra load ... pushes all five over the deadline together" assert conclusions the supplied logs do not establish. The two failed runs killed the children, so their eventual outcome is unknown. The successful probes show slow successful startup in different runs, without phase timings or CPU/cache profiling that distinguishes transformation, module loading/evaluation, memory pressure or another delay. `reviewer-r2-timeout-investigation.log` also records only about 946 MiB free physical memory. Describe resource contention during startup as a plausible hypothesis, explicitly leave the failed runs' cause unresolved, and avoid claiming compilation on every warm-cache start. `startup-durations-probe.log` records six exit codes and durations per run, but no case labels or messages; the unit logs corroborate passing assertions at suite level. The 21.1 s/8.4 s/0.6 s/3.5 s/26.1 s measurements have no supporting command/output in these supplied probe logs: label them as Builder-reported measurements unless their evidence can be supplied. Correct the claim that both reviewer failures had a 45.48 s file time: the second reports 45.734 s. These wording changes retain the proposed fix while making its justification auditable.

- S1-2 [suggestion] Rename the acceptance criterion "A forced hang still fails" to a forced-deadline/kill-path check. A 1 ms deadline exercises the existing timer and `timedOut` assertion against normal startup; it does not inject a real hang. Keep the expected assertion failure and require restoration of the temporary value before final checks. A separate hang fixture is unnecessary for this narrowly scoped constant change.
